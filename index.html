FSCA Regulatory Examinations

RE1 & RE5 — Everything You Need to Know

The Regulatory Examinations are prescribed under Board Notice 194 of 2017 (FAIS Act). They are administered exclusively by Moonstone Business School and are mandatory for all financial services professionals in South Africa.

RE1 — REGULATORY EXAM 1

Key Individuals

For FSPs & Key Individuals — All Categories (I, II, IIA, III, IV)

The RE1 is mandatory for all Key Individuals (KIs) — natural persons responsible for managing and overseeing an FSP's financial services activities. It covers the regulatory framework from a management and governance perspective, including licensing, fit and proper requirements, compliance management, GCOC, FICA and AML obligations.

RE5 — REGULATORY EXAM 5

Representatives

For All Representatives — All Categories of FSPs

The RE5 is mandatory for all Representatives who render financial services to clients on behalf of an FSP. It covers the regulatory framework, your daily GCOC obligations, financial products, FICA compliance, the FAIS Ombud and debarment — from an operational representative's perspective.

10 Expert Tips for Passing
Your RE Exam

Proven preparation strategies from ConnieM Private Tutor. Follow these and maximise your chances of passing first time — for both RE1 and RE5.

01

Master the Regulatory Structure First

Before memorising details, understand the big picture: FAIS Act → FSR Act → GCOC → FICA. Know who does what and how all the legislation connects. Every detail fits into this framework.

02

Memorise Critical Dates & Timeframes

Section 7 effective: 30 Sep 2004 | Material changes to FSCA: 15 days | CTR: 3 days | Records: 5 years | FSCA replaced FSB: 1 Apr 2018 | Debarment notification: 15 days. Make a flashcard.

03

Know the Four Fit & Proper Pillars

Honesty & Integrity, Competence, Operational Ability, Financial Soundness. For every scenario question, map the issue to the correct pillar — this appears in multiple exam questions.

04

Master Every Role-player's Responsibilities

FSCA licenses. FSP holds licence. KI manages. Representative advises clients. CO monitors compliance. FAIS Ombud resolves disputes. For 'who is responsible?' questions — map to the right role.

05

Do Mock Exams Under Real Conditions

Close your notes. Set a timer. Aim for 80%+ in mocks before booking the real exam. Moonstone questions are fully randomised — no two papers are the same. Practice is the only preparation.

06

FICA Details Are Non-Negotiable

CTR = R49 999 threshold / filed within 3 days. STR = suspicion triggers it / no minimum amount / as soon as reasonably possible. Tipping off = criminal offence. PEPs = Enhanced Due Diligence.

07

Learn the Board Notice Numbers

BN 80/2003 = GCOC. BN 194/2017 = Fit & Proper. BN 127/2010 = Compliance Officers. BN 81/2003 = Ombud Rules. These numbers appear directly in exam questions — you must match them correctly.

08

Learn the Debarment Process Step-by-Step

Written notice → opportunity to respond → decision → FSP notifies FSCA within 15 days → appeal to the Financial Services Tribunal (FST). Know each step. The appeal body is the FST — NOT the FAIS Ombud.

09

Watch for 'EXCEPT' and 'NOT' in Questions

Many questions ask what is NOT required or which answer is INCORRECT. Rushing causes unnecessary errors on these. Read every word carefully. Underline key words. Eliminate obviously wrong answers first.

10

Study Daily — Never Cram

The RE exams test understanding, not rote memory. Study 1–2 hours daily over 3–4 weeks. Do practice questions after every topic before moving on. Enroll with ConnieM for a structured, guided approach.
